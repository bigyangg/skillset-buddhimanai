# Product Hunt Launch

## What This Does

Plan and execute a successful Product Hunt launch from 30 days out to post-launch. Covers launch preparation, asset creation, maker-specific tactics, community outreach, and post-launch momentum.

## Non-Negotiables

- **Launch on a Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday.** Weekends get buried. Monday is competitive. Tue–Thu is the sweet spot.
- **The Gallery is the pitch.** Your thumbnail, tagline, and first screenshot determine click-through — not your description.
- **Hunt yourself or find a hunter with a relevant audience.** Being hunted by a stranger with no connection to your audience adds no value.
- **Have 50+ people ready to upvote at launch.** Notification email → upvotes in the first hour → homepage visibility → organic upvotes. First-hour momentum is everything.
- **Respond to every comment on launch day.** Every reply signals engagement and brings your post back to the top of the comments section.

## Timeline

### 30 Days Out

- [ ] Create your Product Hunt account if needed (accounts < 2 weeks old cannot hunt)
- [ ] Begin engaging on PH — comment on other products, upvote genuinely
- [ ] Build your pre-launch email list (newsletter, waitlist, existing users)
- [ ] Draft tagline (< 60 chars), description (< 260 chars), and long description

### 14 Days Out

- [ ] Prepare all gallery assets (see Asset Specs below)
- [ ] Record a 2–3 min demo video (optional but high-impact)
- [ ] Write your Maker Comment (first comment on your own listing)
- [ ] Set up a dedicated landing page for PH traffic with a special offer or CTA
- [ ] Draft the outreach email/message to your warm network

### 7 Days Out

- [ ] Schedule your listing (Product Hunt allows scheduling)
- [ ] Brief supporters: "Vote at this link at [time]"
- [ ] Join relevant Slack/Discord communities that allow product sharing
- [ ] Prepare tweet, LinkedIn post, and newsletter for launch day

### Launch Day

- [ ] Post goes live at 12:01 AM PST
- [ ] Send outreach at 8–9 AM PST (higher engagement than midnight)
- [ ] Post in communities you've been active in (not cold outreach)
- [ ] Respond to every comment within 30 minutes
- [ ] Share milestone updates ("We hit #3 of the day — thank you!")
- [ ] Engage maker Q&A throughout the day

### Post-Launch (48 Hours)

- [ ] Thank supporters in your newsletter
- [ ] Share results + lessons learned post on social
- [ ] Follow up with new sign-ups from PH traffic
- [ ] Apply for Product Hunt Golden Kitty (December – yearly awards)

## Asset Specifications

| Asset | Dimensions | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Logo/icon | 240×240px | PNG, no text, stands alone |
| Thumbnail | 1270×760px | Used in gallery — most seen asset |
| Gallery images | 1270×760px | 3–5 recommended |
| OG image | 1200×630px | Used in link previews |
| Demo video | 16:9, 2–3 min | Loom or MP4, subtitles required |

## Writing the Tagline

```
Formula: [Category] + [Key Differentiator] + [User Benefit]

Examples:
"The Kanban board that lives in your terminal" (Taskly)
"AI code review that explains every suggestion" (ReviewBot)
"API testing without writing a single line of code" (Requestly)

Anti-patterns:
"Revolutionary AI-powered productivity suite" ← generic
"The best task manager ever built" ← unsubstantiated
"An innovative solution for modern teams" ← meaningless
```

## What Makes Products Win

Based on successful launches:

1. **A very specific niche beats "for everyone".** "For solo founders doing cold outreach" > "email tool for businesses"
2. **The video thumbnail gets clicks.** Make it show the product in action, not a logo on a gradient.
3. **A genuine problem story in the Maker Comment.** "I lost $30k to a bug I couldn't catch" → 10x more engagement than "We're excited to announce..."
4. **A special PH-only offer.** Upvoters convert better when there's an incentive tied to the launch.
5. **Reciprocity.** Upvoting and commenting on others' launches before yours is the most organic way to build a network that reciprocates.
