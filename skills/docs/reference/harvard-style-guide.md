# Harvard Referencing Style Guide

Use this reference sheet to format in-text citations and end-of-document reference lists according to Harvard formatting rules.

---

## 1. Core Principles

*   **In-Text Citations:** Format as `(Author Surname, Year)`. Do not use page numbers unless quoting directly: `(Author Surname, Year, p. 12)`.
*   **Reference List Ordering:** Arrange references in alphabetical order by the primary author's surname. Do not categorize references by format type.
*   **Initials:** Use author initials only, never full first names.
*   **Publisher Details:** Format as `City: Publisher`.

---

## 2. Common Source Examples

### Book (Single Author or Editor)
*   **In-Text Citation:** 
    > (Galloway, 2017)
*   **Reference Format:** 
    > Author Surname, Initials. (Year of Publication) *Title of book*. Edition (if not first). Place of Publication: Publisher.
*   **Reference List Entry:** 
    > Galloway, S. (2017) *The Four: The Hidden DNA of Amazon, Apple, Facebook, and Google*. London: Bantam Press.

---

### Journal Article (Academic Journal)
*   **In-Text Citation:** 
    > (Bryson and Crosby, 1996)
*   **Reference Format:** 
    > Author Surname, Initials. (Year of Publication) 'Title of article', *Title of Journal*, Volume(issue number), page numbers.
*   **Reference List Entry:** 
    > Bryson, J.M. and Crosby, B.C. (1996) 'Planning and the Design of Administrative Systems', *Public Administration Review*, 56(4), pp. 339-348.

---

### Website / Online Document
*   **In-Text Citation:** 
    > (Statista, 2024)
*   **Reference Format:** 
    > Author Surname, Initials or Organization Name. (Year of Publication/Last Update) *Title of Web Page*. Available at: URL (Accessed: Day Month Year).
*   **Reference List Entry:** 
    > Statista. (2024) *Global Smartphone Market Share by Quarter*. Available at: https://www.statista.com/statistics/271496/global-market-share-held-by-smartphone-vendors/ (Accessed: 15 June 2026).

---

### Formal Corporate or Government Report
*   **In-Text Citation:** 
    > (World Bank, 2023)
*   **Reference Format:** 
    > Organization Name. (Year of Publication) *Title of Report*. Series code/number (if applicable). Place of Publication: Organization.
*   **Reference List Entry:** 
    > World Bank. (2023) *World Development Report 2023: Migrants, Refugees, and Societies*. Washington, DC: International Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
