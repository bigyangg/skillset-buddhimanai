# Voice & Tone Comparative Examples

Compare these UX copy transformations to align with professional, human-centric design copy.

---

## 1. Action Button / CTA

*   **Before (AI / Generic SaaS Template):**
    > "Unlock Seamless Collaboration and Empower Your Team Today!"
*   **After (Senior UX Lead Standard):**
    > "Invite teammates"
*   **What changed & why:** 
    > Stripped out empty marketing buzzwords ("unlock", "seamless", "empower") and replaced them with a direct, clear button label matching sentence casing rules.

---

## 2. Empty State

*   **Before (AI / Generic SaaS Template):**
    > "Oops, there's nothing here yet! Your workspace looks a bit lonely. Let's revolutionize this screen by uploading files!"
*   **After (Senior UX Lead Standard):**
    > "No files uploaded. Drag and drop file assets here to start organizing your project library."
*   **What changed & why:**
    > Removed false exclamation excitement ("Oops", "lonely", "revolutionize") and replaced it with a neutral state declaration followed by a concrete instruction.

---

## 3. Account Deletion Warning (Destructive Task)

*   **Before (AI / Generic SaaS Template):**
    > "Warning: Clicking this button deletes database data. Proceed?"
*   **After (Senior UX Lead Standard):**
    > "Delete workspace? This action permanent. If you delete your workspace, you will lose access to all projects, files, and customer invoices."
*   **What changed & why:**
    > Restored readability, clearly detailed the specific destructive risks (loss of project, files, invoices) instead of general statements, and deleted useless filler words.

---

## 4. Input Field Validation Error

*   **Before (AI / Generic SaaS Template):**
    > "Error: Password input criteria failed check. Please re-enter credentials."
*   **After (Senior UX Lead Standard):**
    > "Invalid password. Your password must contain at least 8 characters and one number."
*   **What changed & why:**
    > Replaced robotic validation system jargon ("input criteria failed check", "credentials") with a direct explanation of the condition that failed and how to resolve it.

---

## 5. First-Run Dashboard Welcome banner

*   **Before (AI / Generic SaaS Template):**
    > "Welcome onboard! Leverage the power of AI to supercharge your productivity. Click the button to start the tour."
*   **After (Senior UX Lead Standard):**
    > "Welcome. Walk through our 2-minute tour to see how to organize your daily sprints."
*   **What changed & why:**
    > Stripped out buzzwords ("leverage", "supercharge") and replaced them with a specific, time-bounded benefit statement (2-minute tour to organize sprints).
